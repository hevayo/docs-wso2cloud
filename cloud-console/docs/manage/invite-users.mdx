This article shows you how to invite people to your organization, manage the invitations you've sent, and remove members. You invite each person by email and choose the group they join. The group decides what they can do. See [Access control](./access-control.mdx).

## Prerequisites

- Membership in the **Admin** group of the organization.
- The email address each person will sign in with. It must be the email address of their Google or GitHub account.

## Send invitations

1. Sign in to the [WSO2 Cloud console](https://console.cloud.wso2.com).
2. In the top bar, select **Settings**.
3. Under **Organization**, select **Members**.
4. Select **Invite user**.
5. In **Email addresses**, enter one or more email addresses. Separate them with a comma, a space, or Enter. You can invite up to 10 people at a time.
6. In **Group**, select the group they join. **Developer** is selected by default. To choose, see [Built-in roles](./access-control.mdx#built-in-roles).
7. Select **Send invitation**.

The dialog shows the result for each address. An address that already belongs to a member of the organization shows **Already a member**, and no invitation is sent.

Each person receives an invitation email. To join, they accept the invitation and sign in with the exact email address you entered. They then join your organization in the group you selected.

## Manage pending invitations

1. Under **Organization**, select **Members**.
2. Select the **Pending invitations** tab.

The list shows each invitation's **Email**, **Group**, who it was **Invited by**, when it was **Sent**, and when it **Expires**. For each invitation, you can:

- **Resend** it. The person gets a new link, and the old link stops working.
- **Cancel** it. The link stops working.

If you invite an address that already has a pending invitation, the new invitation replaces the old one, and the old link stops working. An expired invitation can't be accepted. Resend it to send a new link.

## Find a member

1. Under **Organization**, select **Members**.
2. On the **Members** tab, search for the member.

The list shows each member's **Name**, **Email**, and **Group**.

## Remove a member

1. Under **Organization**, select **Members**.
2. On the **Members** tab, find the member and select **Remove**.
3. Confirm.

The member loses access to everything in the organization. You can't remove the last member of the **Admin** group.
