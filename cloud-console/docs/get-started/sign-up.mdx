This article shows you how to sign up for WSO2 Cloud and create your organization, or join an organization you were invited to. The organization is the top-level tenant that your users and services belong to. To learn more, see [Organizations](../manage/organizations.mdx).

## Prerequisites

- A Google or GitHub account. You sign in to WSO2 Cloud with it.

## Choose your organization name

Choose your organization name carefully before you start. The organization name and the handle derived from it appear in your default URLs, so pick a name that represents your company or team and that you're happy to keep.

## Create your account and organization

1. Go to the [WSO2 Cloud console](https://console.cloud.wso2.com).
2. Select your region, **US** or **EU**. Your organization and its data stay in this region. See [Data residency and regions](../security/data-residency.mdx).
3. Select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub**, and sign in. By continuing, you agree to WSO2's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
4. Enter a name for your organization. The name must be unique.
5. Select **Create**.

Your organization is ready, and you're in its **Admin** group. The console home page lists the services you can open.

You can create one organization for now. You can still join other organizations when you're invited to them.

## Join an organization you were invited to

If someone invited you to their organization, you don't need to create your own.

1. Open the invitation email, and accept the invitation.
2. Sign in to the [WSO2 Cloud console](https://console.cloud.wso2.com). Select the region of the organization that invited you, then select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub**, using the exact email address the invitation was sent to.

You join their organization in the group they chose for you.

## Sign in with enterprise sign-in

If your organization has set up [enterprise sign-in](../manage/enterprise-sign-in.mdx), you can sign in with your work account instead of Google or GitHub. You don't need an invitation.

1. Go to the [WSO2 Cloud console](https://console.cloud.wso2.com), and select your organization's region.
2. Select **Continue with enterprise sign-in**.
3. Enter your organization handle, and sign in with your work account.

The first time you sign in this way, you join the organization in the **Viewer** group.
